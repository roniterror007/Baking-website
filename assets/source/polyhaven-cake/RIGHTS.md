## Own-render fallback

`cake-scan-v3-render.png` was captured from the site's own live Three.js renderer using the licensed model and the locally computed studio environment. It is a new render of the CC0 asset, not an official website example render. The deployed `public/images/cake-scan-v3-poster.webp` is a lossless 695×442 crop of this 1440×600 transparent original, trimming empty outer margins and preserving alpha with 43 pixels of breathing room. Exact processing and visible-pixel verification are recorded in `poster-processing.json`.
